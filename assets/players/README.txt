Player photos (optional)
========================
CRICQ does not ship any player photos. To show a photo on a player's card, save an image you have
the right to use in this folder, named after the player:

    tim-david.jpg        jasprit-bumrah.png        sunil-narine.webp

(lower-case name with hyphens — or the player's Data Pack id, e.g. 6396.jpg). jpg, jpeg, png or webp,
up to 3 MB. Refresh the dashboard and the photo appears; without one the card shows a monogram.
